💎 Platinum

Risk one copy of an ability: 10% chance it becomes its Platinum version (a better one, yours forever), 90% chance it's gone forever. Only one copy is risked at a time, so extras are the safest to try with.

🔷 Diamond

Risk one Platinum ability: 20% chance it becomes Diamond, 80% chance it stops being Platinum (you get the normal ability back). Platinum abilities with their own special power can be used twice a game as Diamond. The ones that were already "2 uses each round" get something new as Diamond.

    What's new

    Daily minigame

    There's a new minigame every day, at the top of the Play tab. It changes at midnight (Canberra time) and goes round these five:

    • Color Mode: a colour is called out. Get onto it before the timer runs out or you're out. It speeds up every round. Everyone still in at the end gets 30 points.
    • Ultra Boss: everyone in the game against one giant boss. Doors open at 11:15 am, the boss attacks from 11:20 to 11:30 am with lasers, fireballs, shockwaves and by taking away half the map. One hit and you're out. Still standing at 11:30: 120 points.
    • Dodgeball: 1 on 1, one ball. Throw it down your lane and dodge theirs. The lanes shrink and the ball gets faster. Winner gets 50 points.
    • Buildups: one random player is IT, and anyone they touch is IT too. Last one standing wins 100 points. Staying to the end gets you 10 (20 if you started as IT). Leaving early costs 5 (10 if you started as IT).
    • Idle Clicker: click for squares and buy upgrades that keep making them, even while you're away. Most squares at midnight wins 500 points. It starts from zero every time it comes round.

    Color Mode, Dodgeball and Buildups are matchmade, and bots fill any empty spots.

    Diamond abilities

    In the Platinum tab you can now risk a Platinum ability to make it Diamond: a 20% chance it works, and an 80% chance it goes back to being a normal ability.

    • Platinum abilities with their own special power can be used twice a game as Diamond.
    • The ones that were already "2 uses each round" get something new instead. Each card shows what.

    Boss Rush pays more

    Every boss you beat now pays 5 points. Every 10th level pays 50, every 100th 1,000, and level 250 pays 10,000.
