🎉 Original / Casual

The classic game. The winner of each round adds or removes any rule (passive or ability), and everyone gets the room's abilities. No points.

🏆 Competitive

Bring your own 3 equipped abilities. The winner can only add or remove passive rules. Win a matchmade game for +25 points, 2nd gets 20, 3rd 15, 4th 10, the rest 5.

📖 Story

6 levels with new moves you won't find anywhere else. Beat a level for the first time for +20 points. Beat the boss to unlock new packs.

🤖 Bot Grind

Just you against 2 bots, as often as you like. Easy +5, Medium +10, Hard +20, Super Hard +40 for each win.

💎 Platinum
